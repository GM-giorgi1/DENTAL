Frequently Asked Questions

What insurance plans does Premier Arts Dental accept?

We accept 12+ major insurance plans including Aetna, Cigna, Delta Dental, Guardian, Horizon, MetLife, UnitedHealthcare, Ameritas, Anthem, Careington, Dental Network of America, and Principal Financial. We also offer CareCredit, Cherry, and HFD financing, in-house payment plans, and the Premier Advantage Program for uninsured patients. Our team is happy to verify your benefits before your appointment.

Are you accepting new patients in Freehold, NJ?

Yes, we are actively welcoming new patients from Freehold and the surrounding communities, including Manalapan, Englishtown, Marlboro, and Howell. You can call us at (732) 702-2787 or visit our Contact page to schedule your first visit. We'll make sure your experience is comfortable from the very first call.

Do you offer evening or weekend dental appointments?

We offer evening hours on Monday and Thursday until 7:00 PM, which is ideal for working professionals and families with daytime commitments. Saturday appointments are also available by special request. We're committed to making quality dental care fit your life, not the other way around.
